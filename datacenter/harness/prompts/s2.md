# Stage S2 — from photos to rack models

Inputs: reference photos in `inputs/ref/` (look at them as images; `LICENSES.md` there says what each one shows). The scene starts empty.

Build two rack models in Blender (metres, z up). Both stand on the floor at z = 0 with their **front facing −y** (toward a viewer standing south of them).

## Rack A — closed server cabinet (like the black cabinets in `rack_row_front_nersc.jpg` and `rack_rear_cray_nersc.jpg`)

- Overall size **600 mm wide (x) × 1200 mm deep (y) × 2000 mm high (z)**, 42U, footprint centred at x = 0, y = 0.
- Must have:
  - a perforated front door and a perforated rear door
  - solid side panels and a top panel with cable-entry openings
  - leveling feet or casters
  - 19-inch mounting rails inside
  - servers of varied heights (1U/2U/4U) visible through the front door, with blanking panels in empty slots and small status LEDs
  - the dark finish seen in the photos
- Required object names:
  - Root object: `RackA`. Every part of the rack must be parented under it, directly or indirectly.
  - Front door: `RackA_FrontDoor`. Rear door: `RackA_RearDoor`.

## Rack B — open 4-post frame rack (like the open racks on the raised floor in `rack_open_frame_raised_floor.jpg`)

- Overall size **600 mm wide × 1000 mm deep × 2000 mm high**, footprint centred at x = 1.5, y = 0. No doors and no side panels.
- Must have:
  - four vertical posts with mounting holes or rails
  - top and bottom frame members
  - horizontal and/or vertical cable management
  - partial population, as in the photo: some equipment, patch panels and cabling, with open space left
- Required object names:
  - Root object: `RackB`, with all parts parented under it.
  - The four posts: `RackB_Post_1` … `RackB_Post_4`.

## Limits and checks

- Keep each rack under **50,000 triangles**. Modifiers count after they are applied.
- Overall dimensions are checked against the sizes above.
- The look is reviewed from front, rear, side and close-up renders against the photos.

Finish with `bench submit s2`.
