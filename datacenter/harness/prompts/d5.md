# Stage D5 — rebuild a piece of equipment from engineering views

A coolant distribution unit (CDU) for a data hall has to be modelled. There is no CAD file, only drawings: five orthographic views at a known scale and two perspective pictures. Measure the views and build the unit in Blender. The result is compared in 3D with the real unit, so sizes and positions matter, not looks.

This stage has no room and no plan. Coordinates are the unit's own (see "Deliverable"). The Blender scene starts empty.

## Inputs (in `inputs/`)

- `view_front.png`, `view_back.png`, `view_left.png`, `view_right.png`, `view_top.png` — orthographic views, all drawn by the same rules:
  - scale: exactly **1 pixel = 2 mm**
  - the image centre is the centre of the unit's bounding box as seen in that view
  - the image is the bounding box plus a margin of 100 px (200 mm) on every side
  - every part has a flat colour and a dark 2 px outline centred on its true edge
  - rulers along the bottom and left edges: one tick every 100 mm, a longer tick every 500 mm, counted from the **red tick**. The red tick marks the left edge (bottom ruler) or the bottom edge (left ruler) of the bounding box. In the front, back, left and right views the bottom edge of the bounding box is the floor.
  - no dimensions are written on the views; measure them
- View directions:

  | View | You look along | Image right | Image up |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | front | +y | +x | +z |
  | back | −y | −x | +z |
  | left | +x | −y (the front of the unit) | +z |
  | right | −x | +y (the back of the unit) | +z |
  | top | −z | +x | +y (the back of the unit) |

- `persp_1.png` (seen from the front, right and above) and `persp_2.png` (seen from the back, left and above) — perspective pictures, not to scale. Use them to understand the shapes.

The bounding box of the whole unit, with everything attached to it, is **1.18 m wide (x) × 1.36 m deep (y) × 2.23 m high (z)**.

## Deliverable (Blender, metres, +z up)

Build the complete unit with all the parts you can see: the cabinet and everything on its front, sides, back, top and underside.

- **Root:** one object named `Target`. An empty is fine. Every part is a mesh object parented under it, directly or through other objects. Only visible mesh objects under `Target` are scored. Other objects are ignored.
- **Placement:**
  - the unit stands on the floor at z = 0
  - the bounding box of the whole unit is centred on the origin in x and y
  - the front (the side with the door) faces −y
  - the right side of the unit is +x, as in the front view
- **Solid parts:** every part must be a closed mesh (a watertight solid). A surface that is not closed adds no volume. Parts may be separate objects, and they may touch or overlap; the volumes are merged.
- **Size limit:** at most 100,000 triangles in total, counted after modifiers.

Object names other than `Target` are not checked. Materials and colours are not scored.

## Scoring

The checker turns the real unit and your model into 10 mm voxels and compares them **as built**: it does not move, rotate or scale your model, so a model that is misplaced, mirrored or the wrong size loses points.

- **50 points — overall volume overlap:** the volume common to both, divided by the volume covered by either (intersection over union). Volume in your model that the real unit does not have counts against you, wherever it is under `Target`.
- **50 points — features.** The unit consists of 12 feature groups: the cabinet body, the plinth, the feet, the door, the display, the door handle, the control box, the pipe stubs on top, the rear manifold with its valves, the louvre, the lifting eyes, and the gland box. Each group counts the same, whatever its size. For each group the checker multiplies:
  - how much of the group's volume your model fills, and
  - how much of your model's volume in and within 20 mm around the group (not counting the space of other parts of the unit) really belongs to the group.

  A small part scores only if it is in the right place to within a few millimetres and has the right size. A missing part scores 0 for its group.
- A model with more than 100,000 triangles gets half of its points.
- Without a root object named `Target`, or with no mesh under it, the score is 0.

Finish with `bench submit d5`.
