# Stage S3 — place the racks in the room

## Inputs

- `inputs/layout.json` — the data-hall layout, in plan millimetres:
  - origin at grid A/1, +x east, +y north
  - each rack has `id`, `center` (footprint centre), `size` `[width, depth]` and `facing` (compass direction of its front)
  - `aisles` lists the cold and hot aisles as rectangles `[x0, y0, x1, y1]`
- The Blender scene is already open with:
  - the room shell: `Wall_*` objects, `Column_*` objects, and `RaisedFloor`, whose top face is at z = 0.6 m
  - a template closed cabinet `RackA`: 0.6 × 1.2 × 2.0 m. Its origin is the centre of its footprint at floor level, and its front faces −y when unrotated. It stands outside the room at x = −3 m.
  - a template open rack `RackB`, which is not needed here.

## Deliverables (Blender, metres)

1. **Racks.** For every rack in `layout.json`, put one copy of the `RackA` cabinet in the room.
   - The footprint centre goes at the rack's `center`.
   - The front faces the rack's `facing` direction.
   - It stands on the raised floor, so its bottom is at z = 0.6.
   - Name the root object of each copy `Rack_<id>` (for example `Rack_A01`) and give it a custom property `rackId` set to the id.
   - Linked duplicates or collection instances are welcome, to keep the scene light.
2. **Cold-aisle containment.** For every cold aisle:
   - a roof over the whole aisle at rack-top height, spanning between the two rack rows
   - a door panel closing each open end of the aisle
   - every part named `Containment_*`
3. **Floor tiles.** The raised floor uses 600 × 600 mm tiles on a grid aligned to the plan origin (tile edges at multiples of 600 mm). In every grid cell that lies completely inside a cold aisle, place a perforated tile:
   - one object per tile, named `PerfTile_*`
   - thin, with its top face at z = 0.6
4. **Cable trays.** Above each rack row, add an overhead cable tray:
   - 300 mm wide, centred over the row's centreline
   - running the full length of the row
   - its bottom 300 mm above the rack tops
   - named `CableTray_*`
5. **No collisions.** Racks must not overlap each other, the walls or the columns.

The templates can stay where they are or be deleted; they are not scored.

Finish with `bench submit s3`.
